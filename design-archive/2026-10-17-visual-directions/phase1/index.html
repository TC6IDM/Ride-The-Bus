The read

Inside a round the game is well made: results wait for the card to turn, a slam finishes everything in flight, and the held last card is a real set piece. What reads as unfinished is everything around a round: the first second, the hand-offs between screens, the space outside the table, and the chrome in the control bar.

  1. The loader is the one screen every audit flagged first. It is a stock centred splash whose bar stalls at 90% and whose cards never land.
  2. The intro leaves in a single frame. 80ms after Continue the full board is already there, and the cup, chips, deck and die pop in with it.
  3. The guess squares move from optional to rebuild. Stake names "mismatched art styles" as a cause of low ratings, and flat app tiles on a table of lit objects is exactly that.

1

AThe loader

The first frame of the game is a stock centred splash on plain felt: a logo, a title, four cards and a bar, with no table and nowhere for the cards to go.

The bar races to 90% on a 1.4s timer and then sits there, silent, for the whole sign-in wait. One capture showed it unchanged for 4.3s, which reads as hung. The cards breathe on an endless loop. When it clears, an unrelated intro rises over it and the logo jumps to a new place. Replay keeps its own path: load, then the round details, then Play.

EThe guess squares

The one control the player touches most is a set of flat app-icon tiles on a table of lit, sampled objects. The Inside/Outside marks cannot be read without the intro.

Stake's quality rankings name "mismatched art styles" and "border effects" as causes of a low rating, which moves this from optional to earned. Your calls, raised by the audit

  • The critique suggests switching on a tap for the 1× families and keeping the sheet for Three of a Kind. It changes a compliance step, so it is yours to decide; G is designed either way.

  • PRODUCT.md names the bus, the ticket and the destination sign as the world, yet the loader, the intro and the takeover carry no bus.
  • Phase 2 can test it in A, C and the takeover. A new mechanic or prize is out of scope.
