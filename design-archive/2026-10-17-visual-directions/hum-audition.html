The last-card hum

The suspense sound under the squeezed last card, six ways. Each plays at the game's real timing for the stake you pick: on Max, 1.65 seconds up, 0.4 at the top, then the card turns and the strike and the card's own chime land together. Everything is synthesised live through the game's own chain (the sound at 75%, the small room, the limiter), so use speakers or headphones.

Below them, the part you said was annoying: what the hum does while a player peels the card by hand, which can take as long as they like. Pick a sound above, then try each waiting style with it.
