Pick a direction for each piece

Every direction is a working mock-up running on the game's own table, tokens, fonts, cards and chips, photographed off the live build and placed on its measured slots. Each one is shown live at desktop and iPhone 14 size. Nothing here touches shipping source. My recommendation in each tab is marked, and the choice is yours.

What only this game has

  • The busThe route line under the cards with its four stops and terminus, the bus that rides it, and Last Stop's punched ticket.
  • The destination blindMODE, lettered on a recessed sign in the family's colour, with seven bolts.
  • The bar tableOval wood under one lamp from the upper right, the red party cup, chip stacks, the deck, the die.
  • The houseTakeover Casino: the crown-over-spade chip, and the lettered backs on every card.
  • The deckEnglish-pattern courts with the crown, the house Ace of Spades, red or black and never both.
  • The four guessesColour, Higher / Lower, Inside / Outside and Suit, with "=" on the seam, all picked before the deal.

Assumed rather than asked (your brief allowed one question round, and the Phase 1 answers covered it): every direction keeps your settled calls (the deal disc, "=" on the seam, no dimming on a pick, the ticket over cards 2-3, the two-row phone bar). Money, the maths and the copy Stake requires stay exactly as they are; where a direction needs a new string, it says so.

The other six panels

One rule would tie them together. A menu that holds a single control becomes a bump: a small pop-over grown out of the button that opened it, with the board still in view. A menu people read stays a full panel. Every bump keeps the colour of the button that opened it, as the panels do now. That is a design.md change, and yours to make.

  • Turbo · redoA centred dialog for one slider. Your bump idea is the pick (T1).
  • Sound · touch-upBecomes a bump. The boxed speakers go bare (touch-up 2).
  • Bet · touch-upThe chips are already good; they move into a tray that rises from the bet display.
  • Recent rounds · touch-up and a bugA round that kept money prints no result word ("Kept" is missing). Becomes a drawer.
  • Autoplay · redo the layoutAbout 19 controls in one sheet. Counts first, stop rules folded behind a summary, Stake's confirmation kept.
  • How to play · redo the structureThe text stays word for word (Stake reads it). It gains sections you can jump to, and pictures of the guesses.
