# The two characters - masters

Generated by the owner with ChatGPT image generation ("GPT 6 Astra", the
owner's ChatGPT Pro account) on 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-22, from the prompts in
the repo-root `CHARACTER.MD`, and downloaded unmodified. Nothing here ships:
the game gets trimmed WebP files made from these, under the app's `static/`.

**Still to record before shipping:** a PDF of OpenAI's Terms of Use as they
stood on the generation date, saved to `licence-evidence/terms/`, and the
`ASSET_LICENCES.md` rows - the same evidence the Suno track has.

## What was picked, and why

Selected 2026-10-22, by looking at every image on the room's walnut, by a
pixel diff of each frame against its master, and by a mock of both masters in
the 1200x675 game.

### The Boss (right, seated) - being regenerated

The owner is regenerating the whole Boss (2026-10-22) with the cigar in his
FAR hand: behind the table, the near-hand cigar capped him at 220-240 px fully
on screen, where far-hand he can be about 300 px. The new set lands in
`boss/` under the names in CHARACTER.MD's Chat 1 table.

**The new master is `boss-idle.png` (was Boss-Master-1, picked 2026-10-22).**
Both takes put the cigar where it was asked - far hand, tip over empty
background - and both were mocked in the corner at 300 and 330 px with the
cigar visible. Master-1 wins on size: the man fills more of his chair, so at
the same height on screen his face is larger, and the frames have more pixels
in the face and the cigar hand to work with. Passed on:
`alternates/boss-master-2.png` - the same man and pose, but smaller in a
wider chair, which reads smaller in the corner.

**Alternates (passed on):**
- `alternates/floorman-master-1.png`, `-master-3.png` - near-identical to each
  other, broader, with an orange rim that reads as a glow (the game's "lit,
  not haloed" rule). Master-2 is leaner, so his silhouette can never be
  mistaken for the Boss's.
- `alternates/rejected/floorman-hattip-three-arms.png` (Hat-Tip-1) - first
  picked, and wrong: both forearms are still folded across his chest while his
  right hand is also up at the hat - three arms (the owner's catch,
  2026-10-22). Hat-Tip-2, passed over at first only because its wristwatch
  moved to the raised wrist, has one arm up and one folded, so it replaced it;
  the watch is not visible at the size he is drawn. It stood in until the
  third prompt's take replaced it, and is kept as
  `alternates/floorman-hattip-small-hand.png`: its hand was about 0.6 of his
  face's height, and its left fist was tucked at his side with nothing to
  tuck under.
- `alternates/rejected/floorman-hattip-tucked-hand.png` - the second
  prompt's take: the left hand jammed in at his side, his whole body turned
  to face the viewer.
- `alternates/floorman-lean-1.png`, `-lean-2.png` - they square and bulk his
  shoulders rather than leaning, so a frame swap would jump. The lean is done
  as motion on the idle image instead (the upper body tilted a few degrees
  from the hips). Kept as a fallback.
